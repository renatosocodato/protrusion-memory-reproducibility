Figure 1a, Figure 2a and Figure 3a are schematic. Motions, availability and optical mixing have different observation maps. The numerical checks establish behavior within the stated models. The article cites published observations for context at their point of use. The private contextual citation provides no numerical input to the repository.

`computation/reference_checks.json` holds the scalar and structured checks. `scripts/regenerate_figure_inputs.py` compares every input set used by the R renderers. This includes the full held-out prediction tables and quantitative panel data. For the autonomous trajectories, the early trace, bounded proxies and late variability are compared and the finite-time exponents have model-specific tolerances.
