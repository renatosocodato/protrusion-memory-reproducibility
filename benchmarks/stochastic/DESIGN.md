# Locked synthetic discrimination design

The earlier equal-area simulation separated the two predicted shape traces at some phases and nearly lost that separation at other phases. This motivates a finite synthetic study of whether the phase dependence survives noise. The calibrated quantity is the distribution of a discriminator under assumed noise, not the camera noise of an individual movie.

Use frames 0–5 of the verified G01 early prefix (source metadata t_rapa = 7). Convert radius using the TIFF scale and center each sector on its own prefix mean. The median sector temporal SD is a within-vesicle fluctuation scale. It includes any baseline dynamics and extraction variation. Eight sectors are descriptive and come from one vesicle. Sweep 0.5, 1 and 2 times this scale, interpreted as the assigned SD of an intervention-minus-control displacement sample. White detector noise and exposure-averaged stationary OU displacement noise with an assigned 20-s correlation time share that marginal variance. H1299 supplies a coarse 300-s clock only.

Hold the two equal-area pulse mechanisms and observation settings from v1.6 fixed. Cross three clocks (1 s, 65.589218 s, 300 s), five onset fractions within a frame (0, .25, .50, .75, .99), two noise forms and three scales. Keep physical amplitudes fixed across all cases. The 300-s stress extends the synthetic record to four intervals. Also test the 130-s near-frame alignment highlighted in v1.6 at the G01 clock. The 1-s case and its covariance are assigned designs.

Whiten the two shape templates with the known covariance, project out a common constant offset and fit each mechanism separately with a nonnegative amplitude. A score for a mechanism is its likelihood improvement over the offset-only model. Simulate 6,000 independent null paths to set a 95th-percentile maximum-score detection threshold. Declare the winning mechanism when its score also exceeds the other by at least two log-likelihood units. Retain unresolved responses in the denominator. Evaluate on fresh independent simulations of 4,000 trajectories per class (zero, direct and mediated) at each setting. Report confusion matrices, null false calls, conditional tagged recovery and exact ideal-observer separation, with finite-simulation intervals where relevant. The benchmark assumes known actin/actuator relaxation rates and timing. It does not estimate these rates from G01 or H1299.
