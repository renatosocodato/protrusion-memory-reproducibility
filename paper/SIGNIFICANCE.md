When the edge of a cell keeps responding, which part of the cell retains the effect of the stimulus? Two models can place that persistence in a protein regulator or in the actin network and still produce identical mixed fluorescence, actin and deformation signals. The models can be distinguished with an additional reporter whose response to actin is independently bounded, if its response time and the sampling interval allow the recording to resolve the predicted difference. This provides an experimental design criterion for locating the slow step in a protrusion.
