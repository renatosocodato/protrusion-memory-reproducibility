When the edge of a cell keeps responding, which part of the cell retains the effect of the stimulus? Two models can place that persistence in a protein regulator or in the actin network and still produce identical mixed fluorescence, actin and deformation signals. The models can be distinguished by adding a reporter with calibrated gain and a bounded response to actin, if the recording resolves the predicted amplitude difference. This provides an experimental design criterion for locating the slow step in a protrusion.
